# OTA Watchdog — 2-Minute Demo Script

**Setup:** App running at `http://localhost:8001` with `BEDROCK_MODEL_ID` set.
May → June cycle completes automatically at startup.

---

## Opening (15 s)

**Say:** "OTA Watchdog is a deterministic agentic intelligence layer for transport
operations. The moment you start it, it runs a SENSE–REASON–ACT cycle and tells
you which vendors have materially deteriorated in on-time arrival — with every
number grounded in the trip data."

**Click:** Nothing yet.

---

## Step 1 — Show the proactive result (20 s)

**Say:** "The startup cycle has already completed. You can see the three phases —
SENSE, REASON, ACT — all green."

**Point to:** The phase pills (SENSE ✓ → REASON ✓ → ACT ✓).

**Say:** "The system ingested three months of anonymised ride data, applied volume
eligibility, computed OTA, and compared May to June. No manual assembly required."

**Point to the metrics bar:**

> 21 eligible vendors · 18 deteriorations · Fleet OTA 46.98% → 41.14% (−5.84 pp)

---

## Step 2 — Open a vendor alert (25 s)

**Say:** "Let me open one of the 18 alerts."

**Click:** Meera Pavlov Travel card.

**Say:** "Every number here is deterministic — computed from the trip records, not
generated by a model."

**Point to Vendor Evidence:**

> OTA 76.39% → 68.53% · change −7.86 pp · 5,294 current trips

**Point to Fleet Context:**

> Fleet moved −5.84 pp · 18 of 21 vendors crossed the threshold

---

## Step 3 — Recorded delay context (20 s)

**Say:** "The system also surfaces the recorded delay distribution — but we are
careful here."

**Point to Recorded Delay Context:**

> 1,666 late trips · NODELAY 1,518 (91.1%) · TRAFFIC 97 (5.8%)

**Say:** "91% of late trips have no recorded reason — NODELAY. That is a
data-quality signal, not an explanation. We show it without claiming it caused
the OTA change. The system uses 'Recorded delay context shows' — never 'TRAFFIC
caused deterioration.'"

---

## Step 4 — Claude operational brief (20 s)

**Say:** "The operational brief below was generated by Claude on Amazon Bedrock."

**Point to:** The Operational Brief section.

**Say:** "The model received only structured evidence fields — the OTA numbers,
fleet context, and delay counts you just saw. It cannot alter those values, cannot
determine whether a breach occurred, and cannot infer causality. Its role is
purely to convert verified evidence into a paragraph a Transport Manager can
act on."

---

## Step 5 — June → July zero-alert state (20 s)

**Close** the modal.

**Click** the June → July tab.

**Click** Run Agent Cycle.

**Say:** "June to July — zero alerts."

**Point to:** The empty feed with the green checkmark.

**Say:** "All 21 eligible vendors maintained OTA within the threshold. This is a
legitimate outcome. The system does not manufacture alerts when the data doesn't
support them. The breach decision is deterministic — the model has no influence
over whether this list is empty or not."

---

## Closing (10 s)

**Say:** "Five milestones, 214 automated tests, full Bedrock integration verified.
The deterministic layer is the foundation — Claude makes it actionable."

---

## Contingency — Bedrock unavailable

If Bedrock is not configured or unreachable, the Operational Brief section shows
the deterministic fallback narrative instead. All OTA evidence, fleet context,
and delay data remain fully functional.

**Say:** "The deterministic agent and evidence continue to function; the application
falls back to its deterministic brief."
